# Dependency and supply-chain review

Inspect lockfiles, install scripts, registries, provenance, artifact integrity, dependency permissions, CI token scope, untrusted pull-request inputs, and build context. Distinguish known advisory evidence from an age or naming concern.

Recommend bounded controls such as pinned or reviewed versions, least-privilege tokens, protected publishing, reproducible artifacts, and reviewable update paths. Do not download suspicious packages, execute untrusted payloads, probe a network, or claim compromise without evidence.
